Built SharkByte Support at the MLH x DigitalOcean hackathon in Brooklyn. You paste a URL; it crawls the site, builds a knowledge base on DigitalOcean's Gradient AI platform, and gives you an AI support agent you can chat with immediately.

The venue closed at 10 pm, so I went home and stayed up the entire night building. The next day I was the last person to present, still getting the project to fully work while the other contestants were in line eating dinner. SharkByte won 3rd place for Best Use of DigitalOcean Gradient AI.
