Won the Beamly Challenge at the Comcast NBCUniversal Hackathon. The theme was "New Forms of Storytelling Driven by Technology": 24 hours to build with content and APIs from NBC, Telemundo, Syfy, Universal Pictures, and partners like Microsoft and Beamly.

Our team built our submission on the Beamly API and presented it on stage.
