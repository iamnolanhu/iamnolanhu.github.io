Received the Shmooze-a-Student Scholarship to attend ShmooCon in Washington D.C. for the second year. ShmooCon was my first security conference; the scholarship goes to 25 students each year to help cover the trip. Tickets sell out within seconds of going online, so getting in at all is its own event.
