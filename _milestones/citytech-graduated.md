Graduated from NYC College of Technology with a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Systems Technology. Honor's Scholar Program member, Dean's List, NSF S-STEM scholarship recipient, GPA 3.5.

The best parts happened outside the classroom. My Honors Scholars project was a Wi-Fi Protected Setup exploit, pushed by Professor Siegel. As vice president of the Computer Information Association I helped grow the club to over a hundred members and started a small security module inside it to share what I'd learned at conferences.
