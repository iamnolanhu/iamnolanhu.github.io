Teamed up with Daniel Gildenbrand at the Hacking with Agents hackathon in NYC and built TwelveSocial. You upload a long video, Twelve Labs AI analyzes the visuals, audio, and text, and a chat assistant finds and cuts the clips you ask for, ready to post.

Around 100 hackers and 20-plus submissions over two days. We won Best Use of DigitalOcean.
