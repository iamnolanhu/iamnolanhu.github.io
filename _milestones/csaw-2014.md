Placed 3rd in the Department of Homeland Security Quiz at CSAW, the world's largest student cybersecurity competition, held at NYU Poly in Brooklyn. My first attempt was November 2013, where I made the final round.

After a year of practicing, our team Catbug won third place. The three of us met at NYU Poly's weekly Hack Nights.
